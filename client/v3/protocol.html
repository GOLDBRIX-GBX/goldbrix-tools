🎯Vision

GoldBrix is a decentralized memecoin chain built on proof-of-work principles. The protocol has no admin keys, no centralized control, and anyone can participate as a miner, node operator, or user.

The chain enables anyone to launch memecoins via bonding curve, trade them, and participate in a transparent fee-burn economy. Genesis launched January 22, 2026 with no premine, no ICO, and no founder allocation.

🔥Fee Model & Burns

Every fee is a real on-chain burn to a provably unspendable address — verifiable by TX hash. 0.5% on bonding-curve trades, 0.3% on AMM pool trades after graduation. 100% of fees are burned. No one profits from fees.

All burns are publicly tracked. Live counter visible at the Burn Tracker page.
📊Price Discovery

The GBX price is derived exclusively from executed on-chain trades. No peg, no oracle, no promise — where no trade exists, the app shows a dash instead of a made-up number.

How price evolves
Memecoins: bonding curve, then the consensus AMM pool after graduation
GBX: executed LP swaps and pool trades — the only price sources
Everywhere: a dash is shown when no real reference exists
⛏️Mining

Mining is open to anyone. The protocol distributes coins fairly through proof-of-work. Mining is permissionless — no registration, no whitelist.

How to mine
1. Get cpuminer or compatible SHA-256d miner
2. Connect to any solo pool (seed: stratum+tcp://<your-node-hostname>:3333)
3. Algorithm: sha256d
4. Use your GBX address as username
5. Earn 0.25 GBX per block (current subsidy)
Fair Launch
No premine. No ICO. No founder allocation. Mining started Block 1 — every coin minted through proof-of-work, accessible to anyone with computing power.
🔐Security

Keys are generated and encrypted locally on your device. Your password is required at the start of each session; nothing ever touches a server.

Security Features
✓ Self-custody — Your keys, your coins
✓ Session-lock — Password required each session
✓ Local encryption — Keys never leave device
✓ BIP39 seed phrase — Standard recovery
✓ No backend storage — Zero server-side keys
✓ Open source crypto — Auditable security
Your Responsibility
Self-custody means YOU control your funds. No one — not even the protocol — can access your seed phrase or password. Back up your seed phrase securely. Lost seeds cannot be recovered by anyone.
