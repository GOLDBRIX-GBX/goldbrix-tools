GoldBrix is a non-custodial wallet and on-chain market for the GoldBrix network, a proof-of-work chain with an on-chain token launchpad.

Keys are generated and stored only on your device. The app talks to a federation of independent nodes discovered through an on-chain registry: there is no privileged server, no fixed host, and no third-party service. If a node disappears, the app switches to another one on its own.

Features:

* Non-custodial wallet for GBX and launchpad tokens
* On-chain token launchpad with bonding curves and endogenous graduation
* Direct peer-to-peer market with atomic HTLC settlement
* Cross-chain swaps settled by hash time-locked contracts
* Works offline; every page and font ships inside the app
